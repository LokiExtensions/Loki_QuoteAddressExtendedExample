## [0.0.1] - 27 November 2026
### Added
- Example field `example_address_field` in the Loki Checkout (below the gender field), stored with the shipping address (or billing address for virtual quotes)
- Show the example field in the checkout sidebar when filled in
- Show the example field on the admin order, invoice, shipment and credit memo pages
- Show the example field in the order, invoice, shipment and credit memo emails (overriding the core email templates)
- Example field in the customer address book table and editable on the admin order address form
